IqraSpace Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

IqraSpace ("IqraSpace", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the IqraSpace mobile application ("App") handles information when you use the IqraSpace Quran Reader.

IqraSpace is designed to provide a simple, peaceful, and accessible experience for reading and connecting with the Quran.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

The current version of IqraSpace does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, or password in order to read the Quran.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through the Quran Reader.

Reading Preferences and Local App Data

IqraSpace may store certain reading preferences and app-related information locally on your device to provide features such as:

  • Your last reading position
  • Bookmarked Ayahs
  • Reading preferences
  • Display and font preferences
  • Other settings necessary to provide the Quran reading experience

Where these features are stored locally on your device, this information is not intentionally transmitted to IqraSpace.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the App, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

2. Quran Content

IqraSpace provides Quran text and related Quran content from an authenticated and trusted Quran data source.

The Quran content is provided to support reading and reference within the App. IqraSpace does not claim ownership of the original Quranic text.

Where translations or other supplementary content are provided, such content remains subject to the rights and terms applicable to their respective sources or publishers.

3. Advertising

The current version of IqraSpace does not display advertising.

IqraSpace does not use advertising services to deliver advertisements within the App.

4. Advertising ID

The current version of IqraSpace does not use the Android Advertising ID for advertising, advertising personalization, or advertising measurement.

5. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of IqraSpace is not designed to intentionally track users across applications or websites for advertising purposes.

If analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices before or when those services are introduced, as applicable.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on operating-system services, content delivery services, or third-party libraries necessary to operate and distribute the App.

Third-party services may process limited technical information as required for their services, subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

IqraSpace does not sell personal or sensitive user information.

We will update this Privacy Policy if the App begins using third-party services that materially change how personal or sensitive information is collected, used, or shared.

7. Device Permissions

The current Quran Reader is designed to operate without requesting unnecessary sensitive device permissions.

IqraSpace does not require access to contacts, SMS, phone calls, microphone, camera, or precise location for the core Quran reading experience.

If a future feature requires additional permissions, those permissions will be requested only when necessary for the relevant functionality and handled in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Where information is stored locally on your device, its security is also dependent on the security of your device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version of IqraSpace does not require an online user account, we do not maintain a personal user account database for Quran reading.

Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until it is removed by you, cleared through your device's application settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of your operating system.

If future versions introduce online accounts or server-side personal data, we will provide appropriate mechanisms for accessing and deleting that information and update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

10. Children's Privacy

IqraSpace is a Quran reading application intended for a general audience.

The current version does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of applications and devices as appropriate.

If our services or target audience change in a way that introduces additional requirements concerning children, we will update our practices and this Privacy Policy accordingly.

11. International Users

IqraSpace is intended to be available to users worldwide.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may provide you with additional rights regarding your personal information.

We aim to handle information responsibly and transparently and to comply with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
