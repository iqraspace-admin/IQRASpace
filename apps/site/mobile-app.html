Mobile App

IqraSpace in your pocket.

A Tajweed-focused Quran reader for Android, built for calm, colour-coded reading on the go.

The Android app is currently in closed testing and is not yet public on Google Play. This page will link directly to the store listing once it launches.
What the app offers

Built for Quran readers

Only features that exist in the app today are listed here.

  • Tajweed-coloured reading

    The Quran text is colour-coded by Tajweed rule, with a reference guide built in.

  • Native Urdu & Telugu translations

    Read the Quran's meaning in Urdu or Telugu, alongside the Arabic text.

  • Listening Mode

    Whole-surah recitation by Al-Afasy, with an Arabic-only or Arabic-plus-Urdu-translation track.

  • Bookmarks & themes

    Light, dark and sepia reading themes, with bookmarks and your last-read position kept on the device.

Installation & availability

The app is Android-only for now, distributed through Google Play's closed testing track while it's being finished. There is no iOS version. We'll update this page with the Google Play listing, supported Android versions and availability as soon as the app is public.

Prefer the browser?

The Quran Reader works on any device, right now — no install needed.
