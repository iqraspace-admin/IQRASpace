Introducing IqraSpace

A quiet place for the Quran — designed so nothing stands between you and the text.

IqraSpace exists to make reading, listening to, learning from and reflecting on the Quran simple for everyone. The experience is clean, respectful and easy to use, with the words of the Quran always at the centre.

Read at your own pace, listen to recitation from five reciters, follow along with translations, and keep learning — on the web or on your phone.

How it works

From first visit to daily habit in four steps

  1. 1

    Open the Reader

    Go to IqraSpace in any modern browser — no setup needed.

  2. 2

    Choose what to read

    Find a surah and read the Arabic text with your preferred settings.

  3. 3

    Listen and follow

    Play per-ayah recitation, or whole-surah Listening Mode with translation audio.

  4. 4

    Keep learning

    Continue with the Learning App, or on the IqraSpace mobile app.
