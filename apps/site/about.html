About IqraSpace

A calm digital home for the Quran.

IqraSpace helps people read, listen to, learn from and reflect on the Holy Quran through a peaceful, accessible, user-friendly experience.

What IqraSpace is

IqraSpace is a digital Quran platform combining a Quran Reader, audio, a Learning App and a mobile app under one identity.

The name draws on Iqra — "read" — the first word revealed.

Why it exists

IqraSpace is a digital Quran space designed to make reading and connecting with the Quran simple, beautiful, and accessible. We are committed to building it with care, simplicity, and purpose.

Our goal is simple: make it easier to read, reflect on, and connect with the Quran.

Who it serves

  • Everyday readers who want a clean reading experience
  • Listeners who follow recitation and translation audio
  • Students learning with a tutor through the Learning App
  • People who prefer to read on a phone

Authentic Quranic content

Accuracy and authenticity are our highest priorities. The Quranic text in IqraSpace is obtained from a trusted and authenticated Quran data source — we do not alter, rewrite or modify the original Arabic text.

Fonts, display settings, bookmarks, translations and other features are designed to enhance the reading experience while preserving the integrity of the Quranic text. Translations and supporting content are kept distinct from the original Arabic text.

Our design philosophy

Restraint. Generous space, calm colour and clear type keep attention on the text. Decoration is subtle and never competes with the Quran.

Every screen should feel quiet, readable and respectful.

Accessibility and improvement

We design for readable text, clear contrast, keyboard use and touch. IqraSpace is refined continuously through user feedback.
