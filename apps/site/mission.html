Mission and vision

Why we build IqraSpace.

A short, honest statement of what we are here to do and how we decide.

Our mission

Our mission is to create a peaceful, easy-to-use Quran experience for everyone. We focus on simplicity, accessibility, thoughtful design, and features that support meaningful daily Quran reading.

Our vision

We envision IqraSpace as a trusted digital space for Quran engagement — bringing together reading, reflection, understanding, and meaningful learning experiences.

Core values

What we hold to

01

Simplicity

A clean, focused experience that keeps attention on the Quran.

02

Accessibility

A comfortable reading experience across devices and screen sizes.

03

Respect

Every feature and design decision is made with respect for the Quran.

04

Reliability

Dependable when you open it, every time.

05

Continuous improvement

We continuously learn and improve to better serve our users.

Product principles

How these values shape the product

Text first

The Quran is the interface. Controls stay out of the way.

Calm by default

No pop-ups, noise or pressure. Motion is minimal and optional.

Accessible by design

Contrast, type size, keyboard and touch are requirements, not extras.

Honest communication

We only describe features that exist. No inflated claims.

Improve with users

Feedback is welcome and shapes what we build next.
